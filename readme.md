🎮 Muneeb Games – Gaming Website

A modern, immersive landing page for an upcoming game, featuring a full-screen video hero, animated sections, and a showcase of playable characters. Built as a front-end project and deployed on Netlify.

🌐 Live Demo

muneebfayazgamingweb.netlify.app

✨ Features
Cinematic hero section – full-screen background video with the "REDEFINE" headline and a Watch Trailer call-to-action
Navigation bar – logo, Products (Nexus), Vault, About, Services and Contact links
About section – "Discover The World's Largest Shared Adventure" with artwork and the Into the MetaGame intro
Character showcase – video cards for Sage, Jett, Phoenix and Viper, each with a short role description and a Coming Soon tag
"More Coming Soon" teaser – an extra video panel hinting at future content
Contact / Join section – "Let's Build The New Era of Gaming Together" with a Contact Us button and image collage
Footer – copyright, social link placeholders and Privacy Policy link
Responsive layout – viewport-optimised for desktop and mobile
